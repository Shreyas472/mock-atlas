How to Use This in Your Project

  1. Watch & pause — sketch the setups as you go.
  2. List components — frame pieces, motors, belts, electronics.
  3. Simulate in CAD before building, to make sure parts fit.
  4. Flash firmware and dry-run before attaching motors.
  5. Iterate — tighten belts, recalibrate, upgrade the extruder.

Tips for Learning Fusion

  • Keep your history timeline clean — suppress / reorder features to experiment.
  • Use joint constraints in assemblies to get realistic movement.
  • Try integrated simulation, rendering, and CAM features early.

General CAD / Cross-Project Tips & Templates

  • File types: .STL, .STEP, .IGES, .DWG / .DXF
  • Constrain your sketches fully — no loose geometry.
  • Use consistent names & versioning (e.g. part_v1.step).
  • Always double-check units (mm or inch) before exporting to CAM / slicer.
